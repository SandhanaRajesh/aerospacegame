# Global Student Aerospace Initiative's Aerospace Career Exploration Game (What's YOUR Flight Path)
The Global Student Aerospace Initiative's Aerospace Career Exploration Game - This is a series of minigames where users learn about the various jobs you can go into in the aerospace field in a fun way by completing minigames that correspond to the careers.

-5 minigames in total
- Minigame 1: Planetary Scientist, jump onto platforms
- Minigame 2: Exoplanet Researcher, click exoplanets
- Minigame 3: Space Journalist, determine reliability of a news article
- Minigame 4: Astronomer, click stars of Orion constellation
- Minigame 5: Flight surgeon, determine health level variations of astronaut


Created by Sandhana Rajesh, co-founder and USA Representative of the Global Student Aerospace Initiative.
Created for the NASA Stardance Challenge

AI Use:
I used AI to understand how to rewire the gameflow from the tutorial to include the educational information screens.
